FAQ

Straight answers.

Do models download after install?

Yes, only if you click a pack. Instant needs 0 GB extra. Fast Image is about 8–12 GB (Lightning LoRA is a real Hugging Face file; the Qwen base is multi-file — scan a folder you already have). Fast Video is the LTX GGUF, about 15–20 GB — not the official 46.1 GB distilled file. Quality Video stays hidden unless you have 16 GB+ VRAM.

Is this ComfyUI?

No. ComfyUI is not the product path and is not in the installer. Power users who already run a sidecar can open Advanced settings. Everyone else gets stock, painted art, and optional in-app LTX/Qwen when weights are Ready.

Do I need a 4090?

No. Instant works on a laptop with no NVIDIA card. Fast packs want about 8 GB VRAM or an Apple GPU. Quality Video is optional and hidden under 16 GB.

Windows and Mac — same product?

Same wizard copy, same caption catalog, same EDL, same fallbacks. Windows is a PyInstaller exe with its own Python runtime. Mac is the native SwiftUI app. Neither wraps the other.

Will it upload to YouTube for me?

Not yet. You get a publish pack to paste. The Upload button stays disabled until OAuth exists. We will not fake a successful upload.

License

The desktop apps are sold as a one-time license per seat when checkout is live. This public repo is the product source. You do not get redistribution rights to the storefront build. Third-party models stay under their own Hugging Face licenses. Pexels/Pixabay clips stay under those licenses; credits go in the publish pack, not burned into the frame.

Privacy

Local by default. Scripts, exports, and optional GPU weights live on your disk (AppData / Application Support). Optional keys call Pexels, Pixabay, or Microsoft neural TTS if you use edge-tts. There is no cloud render farm and no analytics pixel on this page.

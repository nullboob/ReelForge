The problem

Starting a channel is editing, not ideas.

Beginners do not fail because they lack topics. They fail in CapCut at 1 a.m. — captions, stock, music, thumbnail, title. ReelForge is the overnight pass: topic in, publish pack out.

How it works

Four moves. No timeline.

A JSON storyboard plus a deterministic renderer. The language model never moves frames.

01

Topic

Type a brief or paste a full script. Pick faceless facts, listicle, explainer, storytime, product, commentary, or news.

02

Preset

Fourteen looks — the original eight plus YouTube starters. Shorts 15–60s or long-form 3–8 min.

03

Generate

Voice, captions, Pexels or Unsplash or your files, music, grade. Fallbacks so Generate never stalls.

04

Publish pack

Title, description, tags, chapters, 1280×720 thumbnail, SRT. Copy, export, upload when you are ready.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this Mac only?

Yes. ReelForge is a native SwiftUI macOS 14+ app. Local Windows ComfyUI or Ollama can sit on the LAN if you forward the ports. The editor itself does not become a Windows app.

Does my data leave the machine?

Projects live in Application Support. Exports land in Movies/ReelForge. Optional keys call Pexels or Unsplash. Optional localhost calls stay on your network. There is no cloud render farm.

Can I get a YouTube strike?

We never download copyrighted music. Beds are synthesized or come from a local ACE-Step hook you run. Stock still needs you to respect Pexels/Unsplash licenses. You remain the publisher.

Do I need API keys?

No. Generate always finishes with AVSpeech, cards, and a bundled bed. Pexels and Unsplash keys unlock real B-roll. Ollama, Kokoro, ComfyUI are probes — nice when present.

Will it upload for me?

Not yet. Settings can store a YouTube Data API key. The Upload button is disabled until OAuth exists. We will not fake a successful upload.
